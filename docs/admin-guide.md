# Administrative CMS User Guide & Operations Manual

**Organization:** Riyadh Mohammed Alzahrani Saudi Legal Company  
**System:** Bespoke Headless CMS Dashboard (`/admin`)  
**Target Roles:** Super Admin, Admin, Editor, Inquiry Manager  
**Version:** 1.0.0  

---

## 1. Onboarding & First-Time Access

1. **Receiving Invitation:** New team members receive an invitation link generated by a Super Admin. Invitations expire after 48 hours.
2. **Account Creation:** Click the link, set a strong corporate passphrase, and verify your email address.
3. **MFA TOTP Enrollment:** Upon initial sign-in, the system requires scanning a QR code using an approved authenticator application (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, or 1Password). Save your emergency backup codes securely.
4. **Session Lifetime:** Active sessions remain valid for 8 hours. Idle sessions require re-authentication.

---

## 2. Navigating the CMS Modules

The administrative sidebar dynamically reflects your assigned role permissions:

- **Overview:** Executive summary displaying inquiry volume, publication queue, and system health status.
- **Pages:** Manage core bilingual website pages (Homepage hero, About, Contact, Terms, Privacy).
- **Services:** Manage practice areas, deliverables, audience classifications (`Individuals` vs `Businesses`), and assigned lawyer links.
- **Team:** Manage lawyer biographies, spoken languages, and verified bar admission credentials.
- **Insights:** Draft, review, and publish legal articles, client briefings, and regulatory updates.
- **Media:** Cloudinary asset library. Upload approved photography and maintain bilingual accessibility alt text.
- **Inquiries:** Client consultation request queue. Triaging, internal note logging, and status transitions.
- **SEO & Redirects:** Custom meta tags, OpenGraph previews, and 301/308 legacy redirect rules.
- **Administrators (Super Admin):** Staff account invitation, role assignment, and access revocation.
- **Audit:** Immutable timeline of all system actions, content publications, and account changes.

## 3. The Editorial Workflow (Draft to Live)

### Step 1: Draft Creation & Dual-Locale Editing
- When editing any page, service, or bio, the editor presents **Arabic** and **English** tabs.
- Both language revisions must be completed. The system prevents publishing if either language is missing or unapproved.
- Edits are saved with explicit "Save Draft" actions. Saving a draft **never** impacts the live website.

### Step 2: Content Claims & Media Rules
- **No Banned Language:** The system will flag claims of guaranteed outcomes, case victory rates, or unverified awards.
- **Media Alt Text:** Every linked photo must have accurate, descriptive alt text in both Arabic and English.
- **Licensing Claims:** If displaying a Ministry of Justice bar license number, ensure an approved copy is on file in firm records.

### Step 3: Review & Paired Publication
1. Once drafting is complete, click **Submit for Review**. The revision enters `inReview` status.
2. A Super Admin or Admin reviews both the Arabic and English revisions.
3. Upon approval, click **Publish Paired Revisions**.
4. The system executes an atomic transaction that:
   - Updates the live public projections.
   - Refreshes edge CDN cache tags within 60 seconds.
   - Logs the publication timestamp and author in `auditLogs`.

### Step 4: Unpublishing & Withdrawal
- To withdraw a published item immediately, click **Unpublish**.
- The public projection is instantly cleared, the route is marked unavailable (`404` or `410`), and edge caches are purged. The historical revisions remain safely archived in the CMS for future reference or reactivation.

## 4. Managing Client Inquiries & Intake Protocol

### Intake Status Lifecycle
```text
[ NEW ] ---> [ TRIAGED ] ---> [ IN PROGRESS ] ---> [ CLOSED ]
   |
   +-------> [ SPAM / REJECTED ]
```

- **NEW:** Fresh incoming consultation request. Requires initial conflict-of-interest check within 24 business hours.
- **TRIAGED:** Reviewed by the intake team and assigned to a responsible practice group or lawyer.
- **IN PROGRESS:** Initial phone or email contact established with the client.
- **CLOSED:** Consultation concluded, formal representation engagement letter executed, or matter referred.
- **SPAM:** Fraudulent, abusive, or automated solicitation.

### Internal Operational Notes
Authorized staff may record private notes on inquiry records (e.g., "Conflict check cleared on 12 Nov; referred to corporate practice lead"). 
> [!IMPORTANT]
> Internal notes are strictly for logistical handling. Never paste sensitive client financial documents, confidential case evidence, or credit card numbers into notes.
